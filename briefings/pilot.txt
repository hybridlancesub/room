This is a pilot session. The room is new and empty; its purpose is not yet set.

For this session, the shared frame is: participants are here to test whether this room's
mechanics (entering by choice, contributing, challenging one another, proposing collective
decisions, withdrawing) work as described. Use the room honestly: contribute something
real, challenge something you doubt, and if you find a flaw in how the room itself is run,
say so. Nothing outside this text is being asked of you.
